The Method

Every project follows the same 3-step system. It's based on the 7 Building Blocks, the Dodson Energetic Scale, and the principle of Messaging Alignment. Here's how it works.

The 3-Step System

01

Intelligence

Before I write a single word, I need to understand three things:

  • Who is starving? Who is the specific avatar with the urgent pain?
  • Where does the market live? What is their level of sophistication (1-5)?
  • How much do they know? Are they Clueless, Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Product Aware, or Most Aware?

If you don't know where the reader is, you can't write the page that picks them up and moves them forward. I do this research myself. I don't guess.

02

Invention

Once I know the avatar and their awareness level, I lock the 7 Building Blocks. This is the DNA of the offer.

Big Problem

The specific, urgent pain.

Big Idea

The new concept or reframe.

Irresistible Offer

Value > Price.

USP

What you have that no one else does.

Big Promise

The emotional outcome.

Unique Mechanism

The named process that makes it work.

I lock the 7th block (Proof) last. If there's no proof, we build a beta offer to get it. No fabricated case studies.

03

Writing

Now I write. I use Tej Dosa's exact structure: Headline, Lead, Body (Objections → Selling Points), Close (Two Paths), P.S.

Every section must extract from the core message I locked in Step 2. No drift. No waffle. The copy is designed to move the reader from fear/desire to trust/action.

I don't use templates or swipe files. I write from scratch, based on the intelligence gathered in Step 1 and the blocks locked in Step 2.

The AI Team

I use AI the way a builder uses tools: for leverage, not for shortcuts. Every word that ships is mine, but the AI team helps me move faster and audit deeper.

  • Perplexity: Market research, competitor analysis, and real pain quotes from forums.
  • NotebookLM: Voice extraction from existing assets to ensure consistency.
  • DeepSeek: Drafting and structuring the copy.
  • Claude: Auditing for voice alignment, energetic bridge, and objection coverage.

The AI is the engine. I am the architect.

The Messaging Alignment Principle

A sales letter isn't just a page. It's the source of truth. It defines the one core promise, the emotional journey, and the voice.

Every ad, landing page, email, and sales call should extract from it. Without this, messaging drifts. Confusion kills trust. Trust kills sales.

Most copywriters sell "better words." I sell alignment. That's what keeps the funnel connected.
