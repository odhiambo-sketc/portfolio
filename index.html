What I can show you, and what I can’t

You can’t know this will produce clients, and nobody honest can promise it. I don’t have a case study of my own yet, which is why the first two firms get a founding-client rate.

The closest evidence I’ve found is a case study from The Bottom Line Company about Nguyen & Company, CPA. They moved from ad-hoc follow-up to a systematic process and reported a signed-engagement rate rising from 20% to 84%, as part of $250,000 in new annual recurring revenue over six months. I’m showing it because it supports a principle: consistent, systematic follow-up recovers work that would otherwise go quiet. I’m not showing it so you’ll expect those numbers.

Before you apply
Won’t this annoy my clients?

Nothing goes out that you haven’t read and approved, and every note comes from your address, in your voice. Replies go straight to your inbox.

We get all our business from referrals.

Most good practices do, and I’m not asking you to change that. Referrals bring new compliance clients. The Engine starts conversations with people who already know your name.

We tried a CRM and nobody used it.

CRMs ask busy people to log data every day. This asks nobody to log anything. It runs from one list, replies land in your inbox, and bookings land in your calendar.

Your client data
What happens to my client data?

The export is stored on an encrypted drive, used only to build your lists and sequences, and deleted within 30 days of launch or on your request, whichever comes first. I sign a confidentiality agreement before you send anything. You remain the data controller, and I process the export only on your instruction.

Is this compliant?

I’m not a CPA or a lawyer, and I don’t certify that any campaign is compliant. In the US, IRC §7216 limits how tax return information can be used to promote other services. We scope each campaign around your consent language and your counsel’s advice before anything is built.

Results and time
How do I know this will produce clients?

You don’t, and nobody honest can promise it. I can promise what I control: the build goes live within 30 days, and you get real numbers. Booked calls are the first number to watch. Whether a call becomes a retainer depends on your conversation with the client.

How much of my time does it take?

Before launch: your client list, a 20-minute onboarding call and a 20-minute call to approve the copy. After launch, your time goes to the review calls clients book.
