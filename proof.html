The Diagnostic Framework

When I look at a page, I don't guess. I run it through a 7-point audit. Most pages fail on at least three of these pillars. Finding out which three is the first step to fixing the leak.

  • 1. Big ProblemDoes the page name the specific pain?
  • 2. Big IdeaIs there a reframe that changes perspective?
  • 3. Irresistible OfferIs the value clearly greater than the price?
  • 4. USP ClarityIs the unique mechanism obvious?
  • 5. Big PromiseIs the outcome specific and time-bound?
  • 6. Unique MechanismIs the "how" visible and named?
  • 7. ProofIs there evidence that builds trust?
  • + Energetic HandshakeDoes it meet the reader where they are?
