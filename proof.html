The seven-point audit

Every page I look at gets scored against the same seven pillars. Most pages are strong on two or three and weak on the rest. Finding the weak ones is the work.

  • 1. Big ProblemDoes the page name the specific pain the buyer already feels?
  • 2. Big IdeaIs there a reframe that changes how they see the solution?
  • 3. Irresistible OfferIs what they get clearly worth more than the price?
  • 4. USPIs it obvious what you have that competitors don't?
  • 5. Big PromiseIs the outcome specific, and is there a timeframe?
  • 6. Unique MechanismIs the how visible and named, or left to the reader to guess?
  • 7. ProofIs there evidence that a skeptical buyer would accept?
